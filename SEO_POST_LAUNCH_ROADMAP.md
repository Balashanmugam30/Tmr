## 2. Phase 1: P2 Optimizations (Days 15–45)

### A. Long Product Title Trimming
- **Current State:** 34 of 63 product titles exceed 65 characters due to full trademark names and the `| TMR AI Car Care Tiruppur` suffix.
- **Action:** Review top 15 highest-traffic product pages in GSC. Where SERP truncation obscures the part number, create concise title variants:

  - *Example After:* `3M™ Perfect-It™ EX AC Compound (36060) | TMR Tiruppur` (52 chars)

### B. Short Product Description Expansion
- **Current State:** 35 of 63 product descriptions are concise (< 120 characters).
- **Action:** Expand descriptions with surface-specific compatibility details (e.g. clear coat type, application temperature, microfiber pad pairing) without introducing unsupported claims.
