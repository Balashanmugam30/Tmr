## 6. Analysis of Unsupported / Intentionally Excluded Keywords

Out of the 643 queries marked as "Not Covered", **412 queries (~64%)** represent intentionally excluded or unsupported search patterns:

### A. Low-End / Discount Permutations (Excluded by Brand Strategy)
- Queries containing: `cheap`, `discount`, `low price`, `wholesale`, `cut rate`, `bargain`.
- **Strategic Rationale**: TMR AI Car Care is positioned as an artisanal, high-craft detailing studio catering to luxury and premium vehicle owners. Embedding "cheap car wash" or "discount ceramic coating" would dilute brand equity and attract low-margin, price-sensitive shoppers rather than craft-focused clients.

### B. Wholesale & B2B Distributor Queries (Out of Scope)
- Queries containing: `dealer`, `distributor`, `supplier`, `stockist`, `bulk purchase`.
- **Strategic Rationale**: TMR is an applicator studio and retail seller of verified 3M products. It does not operate as a wholesale B2B distributor for automotive workshops.

### C. Out-of-Market Geographic Queries
- Queries targeting other cities: `Coimbatore`, `Chennai`, `Bangalore`, `Salem`, `Erode`, `Madurai`.
- **Strategic Rationale**: Local search algorithms penalize websites that keyword-stuff geographic regions where they have no physical presence or Google Business Profile. TMR's primary physical footprint is strictly **Arulpuram / Tiruppur (641605)**.
